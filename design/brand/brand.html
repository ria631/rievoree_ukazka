Using it

Keep the sheet limeThe mark always sits on its own lime tile. On lime pages the tile simply merges into the page.
One colour when it mustFor stamps, PDFs or embossing use the mono version, where the X is cut out of a solid tile.
Don't redraw the tearUse the SVG as it is. Rotated or smoothed, the X turns into a plain cross and loses the tear.
